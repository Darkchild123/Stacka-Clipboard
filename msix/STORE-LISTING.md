## Description  *(limit 10,000)*
```
Windows only remembers one thing at a time. Copy something new and whatever you had before is gone for good.

Stacka fixes that. It runs quietly in the tray, keeps everything you copy, and gives it back the moment you need it — right where your cursor already is.

WHAT IT DOES

Copy as usual. When you need something back, summon Stacka with a double right-click, a middle-click, your mouse's side button, Ctrl + right-click, or a keyboard shortcut. A list appears at your cursor. Click a clip and it is pasted. No window to go and find, no reaching for the mouse across the screen.

It remembers text, links, code, colour codes, images, files and whole folders — and shows each with a preview and an icon so you can recognise it at a glance.

BUILT FOR REAL WORK

• Pin the clips you use all day so they stay at the top
• Profiles — keep separate collections for separate jobs, each independent
• Search your whole history as you type
• Multi-file clips open a side panel, so you can paste one file out of forty
• Ctrl+click several clips and paste them as one
• Drag any clip straight out of Stacka into another app
• Reorder, remove, or open a clip's folder without leaving the list
• Snippet scratchpad — type a note and save it straight to your history

YOURS, AND PRIVATE

Stacka has no servers, no account, and no internet connection. Nothing you copy ever leaves your computer — there is nowhere for it to go.

Your history and profiles are encrypted on disk with Windows' own protection, tied to your user account. If Stacka spots a payment card number, it shows only the masked form and encrypts the real one immediately.

Prefer not to keep months of clipboard history? Auto-wipe clears it on a schedule you choose — daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly — and always keeps your pinned items.

MAKE IT YOURS

• Dark and light themes, seven hover colours, adjustable text and row size
• Resize the window by dragging its edge; the list follows
• Every keyboard shortcut is rebindable
• Two icon packs
• Seven languages: English, Français, Español, Italiano, Русский, 中文, 한국어

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE

Stacka is completely free — no ads, no trial, no paid tier, nothing held back. It is released under the GNU GPL v3, so anyone can read exactly what it does:

https://github.com/Darkchild123/Stacka-Clipboard

Built by one developer who wanted a clipboard that actually remembers. If it saves you time, there is a Support button inside — entirely optional.
```
