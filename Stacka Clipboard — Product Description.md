# Stacka Clipboard

**Your clipboard. Always within reach.**

Stacka Clipboard is a powerful Windows clipboard manager that runs quietly in the background and launches directly from the system tray. It gives you instant access to everything you copy, while keeping your clipboard history organized, searchable, customizable, and entirely on your computer.

## Carry Your Clipboard With Your Cursor

Stacka brings your clipboard history directly to wherever your mouse pointer is.

Use a customizable mouse or keyboard trigger to instantly open the Stacka popup at your cursor location. **Double Right-Click** is enabled by default, giving you quick access without interrupting your workflow.

You can also configure custom keyboard shortcuts to perform different Stacka functions.

## Everything You Copy, Organized

Stacka keeps a history of your copied content so you don't have to repeatedly switch between applications or recopy information.

- Store and manage text, files, folders, and other copied content.
- Search your entire clipboard history using letters, words, numbers, or characters.
- Instantly find clips containing matching content.
- Pin important clips so they stay easily accessible.
- Reorder clips by moving them up or down the list.
- Remove clips whenever you want.
- Open or run a copied item directly from Stacka.
- Open the original source folder of a file or clip without leaving the Stacka interface.

## Know Where Every Clip Came From

Every clip has a source.

Stacka displays where your content was copied from, helping you remember the application, file, or location associated with a clip.

No more **"Where did I copy this from?"**

## Work With Multiple Files and Folders

Stacka isn't limited to individual files.

When you copy multiple files or an entire directory, Stacka intelligently groups them into a multi-file clip and opens a convenient side panel for managing their contents.

You can:

- Paste the entire collection at once.
- Select specific files from the collection to paste.
- Copy and paste complete directories.
- Open individual files directly.
- Open the source directory of the clip.

## Select Exactly What You Want

When you have multiple clips available, you don't have to paste everything.

**Ctrl + Click** lets you select specific clips from your history and paste only the items you want.

## Drag and Drop

Prefer direct manipulation?

Simply drag a clip from Stacka and drop it wherever you want.

Stacka is designed to fit naturally into the way you already use Windows.

## Profiles and Groups

Keep different types of clipboard content organized with profiles and groups.

Create separate collections for different workflows and move or share clips between profiles and groups whenever you need them.

Whether you're organizing work materials, development snippets, frequently used files, or personal content, Stacka lets you structure your clipboard your way.

## Preview Before You Paste

Text clips can be previewed directly inside Stacka before you paste them.

This gives you an extra layer of confirmation when working with long text, code, commands, URLs, or other important content.

## Search Your Entire Clipboard History

Stacka includes a built-in search bar that lets you search through your entire clipboard history.

Search using:

- Words
- Letters
- Numbers
- Characters
- Partial matches

Any clip containing a matching keyword can be quickly located.

## Make Stacka Yours

Stacka is designed to adapt to your workflow.

Customize the interface with:

- Themes
- Accent colors
- Mouse triggers
- Keyboard shortcuts
- Clipboard history limits
- Automatic history cleanup
- Optional floating overlay button

The Stacka window can also be resized by dragging its edges and repositioned by dragging its header.

## Automatic Clipboard Cleanup

You decide how long your clipboard history should remain.

Configure Stacka to automatically wipe your stored clips on a:

- Weekly schedule
- Monthly schedule
- Quarterly schedule
- Yearly schedule

You can also set the maximum number of clips Stacka should retain.

## Optional Floating Overlay

For even faster access, Stacka includes an optional floating overlay button that stays available on your desktop.

Turn it on when you want instant access. Turn it off when you prefer a completely unobtrusive experience.

## Private by Design

**Stacka has no servers. No account. No internet connection.**

Nothing you copy ever leaves your computer — there is nowhere for it to go.

Your clipboard history and profiles are stored locally and protected using **Windows' built-in data protection**, tied to your Windows user account.

Stacka is designed around a simple principle:

> **Your clipboard belongs to you.**

## Built With Sensitive Data in Mind

Stacka can recognize payment card numbers in copied content.

When a payment card number is detected, Stacka displays only a masked version to help prevent accidental exposure, while the original value is encrypted immediately.

Your clipboard contains some of the most sensitive information you work with. Stacka is built to help you manage that information without sending it anywhere.
